## What /explore Does

For any roadmap, shows:

1. **Overview**: What you'll learn and why it matters
2. **Learning Path**: Phases/stages with time estimates
3. **Skills Breakdown**: Core concepts and advanced topics
4. **Tool Ecosystem**: Technologies and tools used
5. **Projects**: Hands-on projects by difficulty
6. **Job Market**: Demand, salary, opportunities
7. **Prerequisites**: What to learn first
8. **Resources**: Official docs, tutorials, courses
9. **Next Steps**: Where to go after mastering
